v0.5.0 — Adaptive baseline, 4-language support, in-app alarm mute, self-diagnostic

• Adaptive local baseline: learns normal jamming level for your location so alerts are meaningful in high-EW environments
• SILENCE button: mute alarm sound for 5 minutes without dismissing the warning
• Languages: Arabic, Farsi/Persian (RTL), Ukrainian added alongside English
• Self-diagnostic panel: checks service, battery, GPS lock, and permissions
• Alarm now fires independently of the Python layer if Android kills the service
