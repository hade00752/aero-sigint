Aero·SIGINT is a passive electronic warfare early-warning system that detects GPS jamming, GPS spoofing, and nearby drone surveillance using sensors already built into any standard Android phone.

Designed for non-technical users in high-risk environments — conflict zones, border regions, and areas with documented electronic warfare activity.

WHAT IT DETECTS

• GPS Jamming — satellite signals are being blocked, your location cannot be trusted
• GPS Spoofing — fake GPS signals are making your phone think it is somewhere else
• Drone Surveillance — unknown wireless devices are scanning the area nearby
• EMF Anomaly — unusual magnetic field detected near you

ADAPTIVE LOCAL BASELINE

In areas where jamming is constant (Gaza, Lebanon, Ukraine), a fixed alarm threshold becomes meaningless. The app learns what is normal for your specific location and alerts you when the level escalates significantly above it — showing "+18% above local normal" rather than just a raw score.

KEY FEATURES

• Alarm fires with screen off — Android foreground service monitors continuously
• SILENCE button — mutes alarm sound for 5 minutes without dismissing the warning
• Languages: English, Arabic (RTL), Farsi/Persian (RTL), Ukrainian
• Stealth cover UI — shows a fake battery app when the real UI could attract attention
• 24-hour event log and activity pattern heatmap
• System diagnostic — checks service, battery, GPS lock, permissions at a glance
• Low-power mode — ~50% less battery usage
• No internet required after installation
• No accounts, no analytics, no data sent anywhere

PRIVACY

All processing is on-device. Event logs stay on local phone storage only. The only optional outbound connection is to NTP servers for time integrity checking, which can be disabled.

LIMITATIONS

This app detects interference — it cannot stop it or predict what follows. Indoors without GPS lock, jamming detection is reduced. The baseline takes 10 minutes to calibrate after first install.
