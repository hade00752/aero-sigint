WHAT THE COLOURS MEAN
SAFE — Blue
GPS is working normally. No interference detected. Keep the app running in the background.
WARNING — Orange
Something is interfering with GPS. Your location on maps may be slightly wrong. Stay aware. Do not rely on navigation.
DANGER — Red
GPS is being actively jammed or faked. Do not trust your phone's location or any map directions. Note physical landmarks now. Move to a reinforced building or underground if possible. Tell others nearby.
WHAT EACH THREAT MEANS
GPS Jamming
Radio signals from satellites are being blocked. Your GPS stops working or shows the wrong place. Maps and navigation are unreliable. Common before air strikes — jammers are used to blind guided weapons.
GPS Spoofing
Fake GPS signals are making your phone think it is somewhere else — sometimes hundreds of kilometres away. If your phone suddenly shows you at an airport, a sea, or a different city, your GPS is being spoofed. Do not follow navigation.
Drone Surveillance
Unknown wireless devices are actively scanning nearby. This can indicate a drone or surveillance vehicle searching for active phones. Reduce your wireless footprint: turn off WiFi and Bluetooth if safe to do so.
WHAT THE READINGS MEAN
GPS SignalSatellite signal strength. Above 35 is normal. Below 25 means jamming or no sky view.
Mag FieldMagnetic field around you. A sudden spike means a large electrical jammer or interference device is close by.
Pos JumpHow far your GPS location jumped in one second. More than 500 metres in a single jump means your GPS is being spoofed.
Clock DriftDifference between GPS time and real time. More than 1 second means GPS time signals are being tampered with.
EMF ConfHow confident the app is that the magnetic spike is real interference, not just a passing vehicle or appliance.
Jam ScoreCombined threat level from all sensors. Above 70 triggers the DANGER alarm.
WHEN THE ALARM FIRES
1. Do not panic. The alarm means a threat is detected — not that a strike is certain.

2. Do not use GPS navigation. Look at real physical landmarks.

3. Move away from open areas. Seek a reinforced building, basement, or underground space.

4. Tell others nearby. They may not have this app.

5. The alarm keeps sounding until the threat drops. When it stops, check the LIVE screen before moving.
KEEPING THE APP WORKING
Battery warningIf the orange banner appears at the top, tap it and set the app to Unrestricted. Android will otherwise kill it when the screen is off.
Low power modeTap POWER: NORMAL at the bottom to switch to LOW. Uses ~50% less battery. Alerts take up to 10 seconds instead of 2.
Test the alarmTap TEST ALARM to confirm the sound and vibration work on your device before you need them.
No internet neededOnce installed, the app works completely offline. All detection happens on your phone.
LIMITATIONS — READ THIS
This app detects interference — it cannot stop it. It warns you when GPS jamming starts, not before it starts. Always be aware of your surroundings and follow official guidance from local authorities. This app is a last-resort tool, not a replacement for evacuation orders or official warnings.
